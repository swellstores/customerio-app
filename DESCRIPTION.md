Send your store's customers to Customer.io as people, and what they do as events, as it happens: orders, shipments, abandoned carts and subscription changes. Trigger campaigns on these events, and build segments on each customer's order count, total spent and marketing opt-in.

Connect a Customer.io workspace with the Site ID and API key of its Track API key. The app finds whether the workspace is in the US or EU data center from the keys. Data goes one way, from Swell to Customer.io, and the app doesn't change your store's records.

- **Customers become people.** Each customer becomes a person identified by their Swell customer ID, with their email, name, phone number, location, marketing opt-ins, order count, total spent and order dates. The person is updated whenever these details change.
- **Every order event.** Order Completed, Paid, Payment Failed, Shipped, Fulfilled, Refunded and Cancelled, with totals, currency, coupon and products: names, SKUs, variants, prices, quantities and images. Order Shipped carries the carrier and tracking code, and Order Refunded is sent for full refunds.
- **Abandoned carts, ready to recover.** Cart Abandoned arrives with the cart's products and totals and the checkout link that brings the shopper back, for carts linked to a customer.
- **Subscriptions, start to finish.** Subscription Started, Paid, Payment Failed, Trial Ending, Paused, Resumed, Cancelled and Completed, with the plan, billing period and amounts.
- **Consent stays in your hands.** Each person carries an email_optin attribute from your store, and is unsubscribed or resubscribed in Customer.io only when the customer changes their email opt-in in Swell, so an unsubscribe made through a Customer.io message isn't undone. Customers who haven't opted in arrive with email_optin set to false, so add "email_optin is true" to your marketing campaigns.
- **Each event once, on time.** Events keep the time they happened and an ID that Customer.io uses to drop duplicates. If Customer.io can't be reached, they're retried for up to 48 hours, and if it refuses your keys, your store's admins get an email.

Setup takes a few minutes. In Customer.io, copy the Site ID and API key of your workspace's Track API key from Account Settings > API Credentials. Paste both in the app settings, save, choose Test connection, then turn on Send data to Customer.io. To send the customers you already have, choose Sync existing customers in the Actions menu. Your test and live environments have separate settings, so you can try the app with a test workspace first. If Customer.io sends your abandoned-cart or order emails, turn off the matching Swell notifications so customers don't get both.
